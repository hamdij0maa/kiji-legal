Hopscaper Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026. Updated 28 October 2026.

In short: Hopscaper does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The game registers your device with RevenueCat, our purchase-statistics provider, under a random identifier that does not identify you.

1. Who is responsible

Hopscaper is published by Hamdi Jomaa, entrepreneur individuel (EI), a French sole trader, 41 B rue des Roches, 45210 Ferrières-en-Gâtinais, France, SIREN 927 699 579 ("we", "us"). Contact for anything in this policy: hamdijomaa.x@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

No personal data. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location or anything else that identifies you, and we do not sell or share data. Hopscaper contains no advertising or tracking code, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Hopscaper includes RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a service that counts in-app purchases. Hopscaper sells nothing inside the app today, so no purchase is ever sent. When the game starts, it registers your device with RevenueCat under a random identifier created on the device, with basic technical details (app version, device model, system version and App Store country), and once per device it lets RevenueCat read the in-app purchases Apple holds for this app. Like any internet service, RevenueCat sees your device’s IP address when the game connects. This identifier is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your Game Center profile, and it is never used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf, on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding our sales (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), under its privacy policy and data processing agreement, which covers transfers outside the European Union, and keeps it while our account is active.

3. What stays on your device

Your progress (best scores, level, gems, unlocked hoppers and trails, missions, records) and your settings are saved on your device so the game can remember them.

4. iCloud

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, Apple keeps a copy of that progress in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so it can move between your devices. It is stored and handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it.

5. Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the game sends your scores, achievements and challenge progress to Apple's Game Center, which shows them to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players (for example your nickname and score on a leaderboard). Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can manage your Game Center profile and visibility in the Settings app › Game Center.

6. Crash reports and App Store statistics

If you chose to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

7. Sharing your score

The Share button makes a picture of your score card. It goes only where you choose to send it, through the system share sheet. We do not receive a copy.

8. Children

Hopscaper is made to be enjoyed by players of all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included; the random RevenueCat identifier described in section 2 does not identify the player.

9. Your rights

We hold no data that identifies you: the RevenueCat record is tied only to a random identifier on your device, which we cannot link to you. Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your device, and that identifier with it; the iCloud copy and your Game Center data are tied to your Apple Account and managed by Apple. If you have any question, write to hamdijomaa.x@gmail.com. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the authority in your country.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If Hopscaper ever started collecting other data, we would say so here and in the App Store before it happened.
