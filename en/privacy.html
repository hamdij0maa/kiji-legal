Kiji Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026.

This policy explains what personal data the Kiji app and its sync service collect, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It applies to Kiji on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not use your data for advertising, do not collect your precise location and do not use your content to train any model.

Analytics choice

The first time you open Kiji after this version, the app asks whether you agree to usage analytics. Analytics stays off until you say yes. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Privacy; switching it off stops collection from that moment and does not affect what was lawful before. Crash reports have their own switch in the same place and are on by default.

Data that never leaves your device

  • Contacts. If you allow it, Kiji reads only your own contact card ("My Card") to offer its photo as your profile picture. This happens on your device. Nothing from your contacts is uploaded unless you choose that photo as your profile picture, in which case only that photo is uploaded as your avatar.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and passcode app lock. Handled entirely by Apple's system on your device. We never receive biometric data.
  • Onboarding questionnaire. Your answers stay on your device and are used to prepare your starter content. If you accept the optional starter note, it may mention your answers, and like all your content it is stored in your own private account.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Where personal data leaves the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with the UK and Swiss addenda where relevant. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your content and account data: for as long as your account exists. When you delete an item, it is marked as deleted and removed from your devices. 90 days after deletion, its content (titles, text, notes, amounts) is erased from our servers; an empty marker with no content (its identifier, dates and position) is kept so that your other devices learn about the deletion. Deleting your account removes everything, including these markers, immediately (see below).
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, your account, content, files, profile picture, device tokens and Sign in with Apple grant are removed immediately, and your analytics profile is deleted. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Purchase and transaction records: kept for up to 10 years after the transaction, as French accounting and tax law requires, but unlinked from your account when you delete it: your account identifier is removed from them.
  • Analytics and crash reports: kept by PostHog for as long as needed for the purposes above and at most as long as your account exists, unless earlier deleted.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted within 3 years of our last exchange.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, and in any case if you are in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • export it (portability): in the app, Settings › Export my data produces a file (JSON) with your content and account data;
  • rectify it: most of it you can edit in the app directly;
  • erase it: in the app, Settings › Account › Delete Account deletes your account and everything in it; or write to us from your account's email address;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest, including crash reports and purchase lifecycle events;
  • withdraw consent to analytics at any time in Settings › Privacy, without affecting what was done before;
  • not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing — we make none.

Write to hamdijomaa.x@gmail.com. We answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you). We may ask you to confirm you control the account.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in France the CNIL (www.cnil.fr, 3 Place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07), or the authority of the country where you live or work.

7. Children

Kiji is not directed to children under 13, and in EU countries where local law sets a higher age for consent to online services (up to 16), not to children under that age without a parent's consent. We do not knowingly collect data from such children. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data travels encrypted (TLS). Your content is protected by row-level access rules so that only your account can read it; images and videos are in a private storage area only your account can reach. Profile pictures are readable by anyone who has their exact web address, which is built from your random account ID and is not published anywhere; the list of pictures cannot be browsed. Server access is limited to us. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
